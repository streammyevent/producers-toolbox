📖 How it works with Google Drive
  1. Install Google Drive for desktop and sign in. Your Drive shows up as a drive (Windows, usually G:) or under Google Drive in the Finder sidebar (macOS).
  2. Click Choose folder… and pick a folder in My Drive or a shared drive.
  3. Every backup is a normal file in that folder, so Drive syncs it. On another computer, choose the same folder (or its Speaker Photo Directory subfolder) to see and restore the backups.
  4. In Chrome or Edge the browser asks for permission again after a restart: click Reconnect folder.
